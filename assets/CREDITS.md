# Asset credits and licences

Every third-party asset in this repository is listed here. Images are downsized thumbnails
(960–1280 px wide, or 500 px for the Golden Man photo) served by Wikimedia; they are otherwise
unchanged. Files under CC BY-SA remain under CC BY-SA.

## Map — `assets/maps/kazakhstan.svg`
- **Source:** Natural Earth, v5.1.2, 1:50m Cultural Vectors (Admin 0 – Countries) and Physical
  Vectors (Lakes), from https://github.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector
- **Licence:** public domain (https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/). No attribution
  is required; credited here as "Made with Natural Earth."
- **Changes:** Kazakhstan, its neighbours and lakes drawn as an SVG by `tools/build_map.py`
  (equirectangular projection, fixed view). Borders are as in Natural Earth and are shown only to
  locate places for the quiz.
