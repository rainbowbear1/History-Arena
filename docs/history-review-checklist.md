# Historical accuracy checklist

The question banks were rewritten in November 2026 (`docs/question-overhaul-2026-11.md` lists every
change; `docs/content-review.md` lists every task with its answer and decoys). Questions use facts
from standard Kazakhstan history courses and widely cited sources; where sources disagree, the
uncertain detail was left out or the wording says "traditionally". The English versions (`en` in
each file) were written alongside and need the same check. Please tick each item after checking it
against the school textbook or your teacher.

## Check first: dates or attributions that sources give differently
- [ ] **kazakh_khanate-02** — the Kazakh Khanate founded in **1465** (textbook date). Some historians
      argue for 1456–1459 or 1470; those years are deliberately **not** among the options.
- [ ] **dzungar-01** — Ақтабан шұбырынды began in **1723**; options are close years (1718, 1720, 1725;
      decoys 1717, 1722). Confirm none of them is a textbook alternative.
- [ ] **dzungar-04** — Аңырақай: the united force is credited to **Әбілқайыр хан** (wrong: Болат,
      Сәмеке, Әбілмәмбет). The year (1729 or 1730) is not asked.
- [ ] **dzungar-07** — «Елім-ай» is attributed to **Қожаберген жырау** "traditionally"; the attribution is
      debated. Remove the task if your course does not teach it.
- [ ] **golden_horde-05** — **Орда Ежен** founded the Ақ Орда. Sources swap the names Ақ Орда / Көк Орда;
      the question follows the Kazakh textbook usage (the eastern wing with Сығанақ).
- [ ] **golden_horde-13** — the Ұлытау mausoleum "is traditionally attributed" to **Жошы хан**.
- [ ] **turkic-14** — **Йоллығ тегін** named as the carver/author of the Күлтегін inscription.
- [ ] **turkic-09** — Арабтар **Сұлу** қағанды «Әбу Мұзахим» деп атаған.
- [ ] **saka-02 / saka-08** — legends and reliefs: **Шырақ** (Polyaenus), **Скунха** (Behistun relief).
- [ ] **saka-03** — **Кемал Ақышев** excavated Есік in 1969–1970.
- [ ] **otyrar-08** — **Жебе мен Сүбедей** pursued Мұхаммед and then rode around the Caspian.
- [ ] **otyrar-14** — **Қараджа хажиб** left Otrar and surrendered (wrong: Темір Мәлік, Хұмар тегін, Ұзлағ шах).
- [ ] **kazakh_khanate-07** — the first European diplomatic contact under Қасым хан is the **Мәскеу
      мемлекеті** (Grand Duchy of Moscow). Not "Ресей империясы", which did not exist yet.
- [ ] **kazakh_khanate-11** — biys' council at **Күлтөбе** near Түркістан under Тәуке хан.
- [ ] **xviii_xix-01** — **Мамбет (Алексей) Тевкелев** received Әбілқайыр's oath in 1731.
- [ ] **xviii_xix-04** — «Ереуіл атқа ер салмай» is **Махамбет**'s poem (the patronymic is no longer in
      the prompt, so it no longer gives the answer away).
- [ ] **xviii_xix-13** — Алтынсарин opened the school in **Торғай** in 1864.
- [ ] **xx_century-01** — the 25 June 1916 decree: men drafted for **labour behind the front** (not for
      combat; "Әскерге шақырып, майданға жіберу" is the near-miss option).
- [ ] **xx_century-02** — the Torgai rebels elected **Әбдіғаппар Жанбосынұлы** khan; Амангелді Иманов was
      their military leader (сардарбек).
- [ ] **xx_century-05** — **Голощекин**, 1925–1933, «Кіші Қазан». The famine itself is no longer asked
      as a one-line question. If it should be, word it neutrally (forced collectivisation and
      sedentarisation are the causes most historians name).
- [ ] **xx_century-13** — **Хиуаз Доспанова**, pilot-navigator of a night bomber regiment (Halyk Kaharmany,
      2004). Мәншүк and Әлия are now wrong options here; the old Мәншүк question was dropped because
      her character card already shows her machine gun.

## Check second: modern history (level 10)
- [ ] **independence-01** — Constitutional Law on State Independence, **16 Dec 1991**. Near misses:
      25 Oct 1990 (sovereignty), 1 Dec 1991 (first presidential election), 10 Dec 1991 (renaming),
      decoys 21 Dec 1991 (Almaty declaration) and 2 Mar 1992 (UN membership).
- [ ] **independence-02** — Alma-Ata declaration (CIS expansion), **Almaty**, 21 Dec 1991.
- [ ] **independence-03** — current Constitution, referendum **1995** (30 Aug); 1993 = first constitution.
- [ ] **independence-04** — OSCE chairmanship **2010** (the Astana summit was 1–2 Dec 2010).
- [ ] **independence-05** — UN Security Council non-permanent member **2017–2018**.
- [ ] **independence-06** — SCO founded **2001** (Shanghai Five: 1996).
- [ ] **independence-07** — CICA proposed at the **47th UN General Assembly, 1992**.
- [ ] **independence-08** — 29 August = **International Day against Nuclear Tests** (UN, 2009).
- [ ] **independence-09** — WTO membership **2015** (30 Nov).
- [ ] **independence-10** — March 2019: Senate chairman **Қасым-Жомарт Тоқаев** became President under the
      Constitution. Wording is factual and neutral.
- [ ] **independence-11** — after the 2022 amendments: **a single seven-year presidential term**.
- [ ] **independence-12** — Senate (upper), Mazhilis (lower), Supreme Council (single chamber before 1995).
- [ ] **independence-13** — order: sovereignty declaration (1990) → tenge (1993) → capital to Akmola (1997) →
      Eurasian Economic Union (treaty 2014, in force 2015).
- [ ] **independence-14** — Tengizchevroil founded **1993**; pin on the Tengiz field (approximate).
- [ ] **independence-15** — the city took the name **Астана in 1998** (Ақмола before, Нұр-Сұлтан 2019–2022).
- [ ] Sensitive events (January 2022, Desember 1986 beyond the appointment of Колбин) are deliberately
      not asked.

## Map pins (approximate coordinates)
- [ ] Pins use rounded coordinates of towns or sites. Wrong pins are now **nearby** places: for example
      Семей beside Өскемен, Павлодар and Аягөз; Отырар beside Ташкент, Тараз and Қызылорда; Сарайшық
      beside Құлсары, Бейнеу and Орал. Pins are at least 0.05 map units apart (about 2.6° of longitude).
