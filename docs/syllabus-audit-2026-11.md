# INN 102 syllabus audit of the question banks (18 November 2026)

Reference: the INN 102 «Қазақстан тарихы» syllabus (Sarsenbaev A., autumn 2026), weeks 1–15, and its
required textbooks. Every campaign task and every medical-help task was checked for: historical
correctness, clear wording, relevance to a syllabus week, and a difficulty that comes from historical
knowledge rather than obscure trivia. Only tasks that failed a check were changed. Facts in new tasks
were checked against the sources listed; where sources disagree (e.g. the year the Karakhanids adopted
Islam, the Kangju capital) the detail was not asked.

The verified pool is used by the campaign, the BOSS (50 tasks drawn from all ten level banks) and medical
help (its own bank). Endless Mode will use the same pool once it is specified.
